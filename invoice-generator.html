Free Invoice Generator for India with GST

An invoice is the bill you give a customer for goods or services. A clear invoice gets you paid faster, keeps your records tidy and is needed for GST. This free invoice generator lets freelancers, shopkeepers and small businesses create a professional invoice in minutes and download it as an A4 PDF. You do not need accounting software or an account. The preview updates while you type.

What to include in an invoice

A good invoice shows who is selling and who is buying. It includes your business name and address, the customer name and address, a unique invoice number and the date. Then it lists each item with quantity, rate, tax and amount, followed by the totals. Payment details or notes, such as bank account, UPI ID or due date, help the customer pay correctly. If your business is registered for GST, your GSTIN must appear on the invoice.

How to create an invoice step by step

  1. Enter your business name, address and GSTIN if you have one.
  2. Enter the customer details.
  3. Set the invoice number and date.
  4. Choose the GST type: no GST, CGST plus SGST, or IGST.
  5. Add each item with description, quantity, rate and GST percentage. Press Add item for more rows.
  6. Add notes such as payment details, and a signatory name.
  7. Check the preview and press Download PDF.

CGST, SGST and IGST explained

When you sell within your own state, GST is split equally between the central and state governments as CGST and SGST. For example, 18 percent GST becomes 9 percent CGST and 9 percent SGST. When you sell to a customer in another state, a single tax called IGST applies at the full rate. This tool does the split for you. Choose "CGST + SGST" for same-state sales and "IGST" for other-state sales. If you are not registered for GST, choose "No GST" and the invoice is titled Invoice instead of Tax Invoice.

Common GST rates

GST rates differ by product and service, so always confirm the correct rate for your item. Many services are taxed at 18 percent, and goods fall under various slabs. Enter the percentage that applies to each item. Different items on the same invoice can have different rates, and the tool adds them up correctly.

Tips for professional invoices

  • Use a running invoice number such as INV-001, INV-002. Do not repeat numbers.
  • Write clear item descriptions, so the customer understands what they are paying for.
  • Add a due date and payment details in the notes.
  • Keep a copy of every invoice you send, for tax and accounts.
  • Check totals before downloading. Mistakes are easier to fix before you send.

Example: a simple GST invoice

Suppose a freelance designer in Pune sells a logo design to a company in Pune for 10,000 rupees at 18 percent GST. Choose CGST + SGST, add one item with quantity 1, rate 10000 and GST 18. The invoice shows a subtotal of 10,000.00, CGST of 900.00, SGST of 900.00 and a total of 11,800.00. If the buyer were in another state, you would choose IGST and the invoice would show a single IGST line of 1,800.00 with the same total. Add your UPI ID or bank details in the notes so the customer knows where to pay.

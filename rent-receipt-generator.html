Free Rent Receipt Generator for HRA in India

Salaried tenants are often asked by their employer for rent receipts to support an HRA (House Rent Allowance) claim. This free tool lets you create a clear, professional rent receipt in under a minute. Enter the landlord and tenant details, the rent period and the payment mode, check the live preview, then download a PDF or print it. No signup is needed, and the details you enter stay in your browser.

What is a rent receipt?

A rent receipt is a written acknowledgement from the landlord that rent has been received for a specific period. It normally shows the landlord's and tenant's names, the property address, the amount, the period covered, the date and how the rent was paid.

Who needs a rent receipt?

  • Salaried employees claiming HRA. Employers usually collect rent receipts or rent declarations during the tax-proof submission window, often between December and March.
  • Tenants who want a record of every rent payment.
  • Landlords who want a signed record of rent received.

Details to include for HRA proof

  • Tenant name and landlord name
  • Complete address of the rented property
  • Rent amount and the period it covers (for example, 01 Apr 2026 to 30 Apr 2026)
  • Date of receipt and payment mode (bank transfer, UPI, cheque or cash)
  • Landlord's signature
  • Landlord's PAN. Employers commonly ask for it when yearly rent is more than ₹1,00,000, which is about ₹8,333 per month. Check your employer's current requirement.

Paying by bank transfer or UPI gives you a payment trail that supports the receipt. HRA exemption also depends on your tax regime, salary structure and city, so confirm the details with your employer or a tax adviser.

How to create a rent receipt

  1. Enter the landlord's name, address and PAN if applicable.
  2. Enter the tenant's name and the full address of the rented property.
  3. Choose the rent period and enter the monthly rent.
  4. Add the receipt date, payment mode and transaction reference.
  5. Check the preview, then tap Download PDF or Print / Save PDF.
  6. Get the landlord to sign it before you submit it.

Example

Priya Verma pays ₹12,000 per month to Amit Sharma by bank transfer. For April 2026 she enters the period 01 Apr 2026 to 30 Apr 2026, amount ₹12,000, and the transfer reference. The receipt shows the amount as "Rupees Twelve Thousand Only". Her yearly rent is ₹1,44,000, which is above ₹1 lakh, so she also adds her landlord's PAN.

Tips

  • Create one receipt for each month, or one for a quarter if your employer accepts it.
  • Use a different receipt number for each receipt.
  • Keep rent payments and receipt dates consistent with your bank statement.
  • Do not enter a fake landlord, PAN or rent amount. False claims can lead to tax notices and penalties.

Frequently asked questions

Is this rent receipt generator free?
Yes. You can create and print receipts without signing up.

Can I use the receipt for HRA claims?
You can use it as supporting proof of rent paid. Your employer may also ask for a rent agreement, bank statements or a landlord PAN. Check what your employer requires.

Is landlord PAN compulsory?
Employers commonly ask for the landlord's PAN when annual rent is above ₹1 lakh. Below that, it is usually optional.

Do I need a revenue stamp?
Requirements differ by situation and state. For ordinary rent receipts, many employers do not insist on one. Ask your employer if you are unsure.

Can I make receipts for several months?
Yes. Change the rent period and receipt number, then download each receipt separately.

Does HisaabKitab store my details?
No. The receipt is created in your browser and the entered details are not sent to our server.

Is the receipt legally valid on its own?
The tool makes a receipt from the details you enter. The landlord should verify and sign it. Validity depends on your situation and the applicable rules.

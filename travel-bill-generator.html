What Is a Travel Bill?

A travel bill is a document a travel agency, cab operator or tour company gives a customer for a trip. It shows who provided the service, who paid for it, where the journey started and ended, and how much it cost. Companies often ask employees for a travel bill before they approve a reimbursement. A neat, complete bill avoids delays.

Tips for a Better Travel Bill

  • Give every bill a unique invoice number.
  • Write the route and dates clearly, since reimbursements depend on them.
  • List each charge separately instead of one lump sum.
  • Add your GSTIN if you are registered.
  • Keep a PDF copy of every bill for your records.

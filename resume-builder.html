Free Resume Builder: Make a Resume and Download PDF

A good resume helps you get the interview. It must be clear, easy to read and short enough that a recruiter can scan it in less than a minute. This free resume builder lets you type your details, see the resume form on screen and download a PDF. You do not need to sign up, pay or install anything, and it works on your phone.

How to build your resume

  1. Choose a template: Classic for a traditional look, or Modern for a coloured header.
  2. Enter your name, job title, email, phone and city.
  3. Write a short summary of two or three lines.
  4. Add your experience. Write the role and company on the first line, then one point per line below it. Leave a blank line between jobs.
  5. Add your education in the same way.
  6. List your skills separated by commas.
  7. Check the preview and press Download PDF.

What to put in each section

Header: your name, a clear job title, a professional email and a working phone number. Summary: two or three lines about your experience and what you do best. Experience: list jobs from the most recent. For each, write the role, company and years, then points about what you achieved. Education: your degree, institute and year. Skills: tools, software and abilities that match the job.

Writing strong experience points

Start each point with an action word such as managed, installed, repaired, designed, trained or reduced. Then say what you did and what changed. Numbers make points stronger. Compare "Maintained electrical systems" with "Maintained electrical systems on 3 vessels with zero major breakdowns in 2 years". Keep each point to one or two lines, and avoid long paragraphs.

Classic or Modern template?

The Classic template uses a serif font and black text. It suits government jobs, banking, teaching and other traditional fields. The Modern template uses a clean sans-serif font with a coloured header, and it suits private companies, technical roles and startups. Both are single-column with simple formatting, which is easier for applicant tracking systems to read than complex designs with tables and graphics.

Resume tips for Indian job seekers

  • Keep your resume to one page if you have under ten years of experience, and two pages at most for senior roles.
  • Use a professional email address, such as your name, rather than a nickname.
  • Add only a phone number and city. Your full home address is not needed.
  • Tailor your skills and summary to each job description.
  • Do not include personal details such as religion, caste or marital status, unless a government form asks.
  • Save the file with a clear name like Name-Resume.pdf before sending.

Common resume mistakes

The biggest mistakes are spelling errors, vague points, unexplained gaps and too much text. Read your resume aloud before downloading. Ask a friend to check it. Do not lie about skills or dates, since employers often verify them. Keep the layout simple and consistent, using the same date format throughout.

Privacy and limits

Everything you type stays in your browser. HisaabKitab does not upload, store or read your resume, so your phone number and career history stay private. The downside is that nothing is saved. If you close the page, your details are gone, so download your PDF and keep a copy of your text. This builder does not support photos or multiple pages with custom columns. It is made for a quick, clean, text-based resume.

Resume example for an electrical technician

If you work in a technical field, keep your resume practical. In the summary, state your years of experience and main strengths, for example "Electro technical professional with 10 years of experience in maintenance and fault finding". In the experience box, write "Electrical Officer - Company (2018-2024)" and then points like "Maintained high-voltage systems and carried out preventive maintenance" and "Trained junior crew on safety procedures". Add certificates in the education section and list tools or systems in the skills box. This simple structure works for most technical, engineering and operations jobs.

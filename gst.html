How GST is calculated

Add GST: price × rate ÷ 100. Remove GST: price × rate ÷ (100 + rate). Within a state the tax splits equally into CGST and SGST; between states it is charged as IGST. Check the current rate for your item on the official GST portal.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

CGST and SGST apply to sales within one state. IGST applies to sales between states.

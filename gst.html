How to use this GST calculator

Enter the amount, pick the GST rate and choose whether to add GST to a price or remove it from a GST-inclusive price.

GST formulas

Add GST: price × rate ÷ 100. Remove GST: price × rate ÷ (100 + rate). Within a state, GST splits equally into CGST and SGST. Between states it is charged as IGST.

Check the rate for your item on the official GST portal, as rates change.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

CGST and SGST apply to sales within one state. IGST applies to sales between states.

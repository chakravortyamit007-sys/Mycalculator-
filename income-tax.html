Income Tax Calculator

How this works

New regime uses a ₹75,000 standard deduction and slabs up to 30% above ₹24 lakh, with tax rebate making income up to ₹12 lakh tax-free. Old regime uses a ₹50,000 standard deduction plus your deductions. Both add 4% cess. Surcharge for incomes above ₹50 lakh is not included. Slabs change in the Budget, so check the Income Tax Department website.

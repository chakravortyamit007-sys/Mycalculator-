How to use this EMI calculator

Enter the loan amount, the yearly interest rate and the tenure in years. The result updates as you type, showing your monthly EMI, total interest and total amount payable.

EMI formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of months.

Example

A ₹25,00,000 loan at 8.5% for 20 years has an EMI of about ₹21,696. Over 20 years you would repay about ₹52 lakh in total.

Frequently asked questions

Does a longer tenure reduce my EMI?

Yes, the monthly EMI falls, but you pay more total interest.

Will my bank show the same EMI?

For fixed rates it should match closely. Floating rates, processing fees and rounding can cause small differences.

How can I reduce total interest?

Choose a shorter tenure, make part-prepayments, or compare lenders for a lower rate.

EMI Calculator

How EMI is calculated

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of monthly instalments.

Frequently asked questions

Does a longer tenure reduce EMI?

Yes, but you pay more total interest.

Is this the same as my bank's figure?

It should match for a fixed rate. Floating rates, fees and rounding can cause small differences.

Free PDF Converter Online: Images to PDF, Merge and Split

A PDF is the file format most Indian portals ask for. Job applications, bank KYC, college admissions, exam forms and government services often want one clean PDF instead of a pile of photos. This free PDF converter on HisaabKitab helps you turn images into a PDF, join several PDFs into one and keep only the pages you need. Everything runs in your browser, so there is no sign-up and no upload.

What can this PDF converter do?

The tool has three functions. Images to PDF converts JPG, PNG and WebP pictures into a single PDF, with one image per A4 page. Merge PDFs combines two or more PDF files into one document, in the order you choose. Split PDF extracts selected pages from a longer PDF and saves them as a new file. Together they cover most everyday document jobs.

How to convert JPG or PNG to PDF

  1. Open the "Images to PDF" tab.
  2. Choose your images. Select them in the order you want the pages.
  3. Press "Create PDF".
  4. Your PDF downloads automatically as images.pdf.

Each image is centred on an A4 page and scaled down to fit with a small margin, so photos of Aadhaar cards, marksheets or certificates look tidy. Large photos are not stretched.

How to merge PDF files

  1. Open the "Merge PDFs" tab.
  2. Choose at least two PDF files.
  3. Press "Merge PDFs".
  4. Download merged.pdf, with all pages in the order you selected.

Merging is useful when a form wants your application, ID proof and certificates as one file. Rename your files 1, 2, 3 before selecting them so the order is easy to control.

How to split a PDF or extract pages

Open the "Split PDF" tab and choose your file. In the pages box, type the pages you want to keep. Write a range like 1-3, single pages like 5, or a mix such as 1-3,5,8-10. Press "Extract pages" and your new PDF downloads as extracted.pdf. The original file is not changed. If you type a page number that does not exist, the tool tells you how many pages the PDF has.

Why use a browser-based PDF tool?

Many online converters upload your files to a server. That is risky when the file is an identity proof, bank statement or salary slip. This tool processes files on your own phone or computer. Your documents are not sent to HisaabKitab, and nothing is stored after you close the page. It also works fast because there is no upload or waiting in a queue.

When do you need these tools?

Common uses in India include combining Aadhaar, PAN and address proof into one KYC file, converting photos of marksheets into a single PDF for admission forms, merging resume and cover letter for job portals, sending only the relevant pages of a long bank statement, and preparing rent agreements, invoices or medical bills for claims. Many portals set a file size limit. If your PDF is too large, compress the photos first with our Image Compressor, then convert them to PDF.

Tips for best results

  • Take photos in good light and keep the document flat so text stays readable.
  • Crop extra background before converting, so pages look neat.
  • Compress very large photos first to keep the final PDF small.
  • Check the downloaded PDF before uploading it anywhere.
  • Keep the original files until the PDF is accepted.

Which PDF tool should you use?

Use Images to PDF when you have photos or scans and the portal wants one document. Use Merge PDFs when you already have several PDF files, such as a resume, certificates and an ID proof, and need to send them together. Use Split PDF when you have one large PDF but only need a few pages, for example the last three pages of a bank statement or a single page from a long agreement. You can also use them one after another: split a long file, then merge the pages you need with another document.

What this tool cannot do

This converter does not turn Word, Excel or PowerPoint files into PDF, and it cannot convert a PDF back into an editable Word file. Those conversions need server software. It also cannot open password-protected PDFs. Remove the password in your PDF viewer first, then use the tool.
